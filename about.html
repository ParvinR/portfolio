Background

Parvin V Ramroop

I'm a Computer Science senior at Queens College (CUNY) with hands-on experience in machine learning research, embedded systems programming, and full-stack web development. My academic work centers on applying Bayesian Optimization to real-world coverage problems under the supervision of Prof. Bon Sy.

My foundation in C++ and Java underpins my interest in low-level, performance-critical systems — the kind of engineering that powers aircraft, defense systems, and satellite infrastructure. I approach problems with a systems-thinking mindset: understanding constraints, optimizing under them, and building things that work reliably.

Outside of academics, I've built a React-based tool for structured multi-AI collaboration, led a group machine learning project on diabetes prediction, and continuously explored the intersection of theory and applied engineering.

Career trajectory: After graduation, I plan to join the Air National Guard and pursue an online MS in Electrical Engineering at the University of Colorado — with a long-term target of an embedded systems engineering role at Lockheed Martin.
