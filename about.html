Background

Parvin V Ramroop

Computer Science senior focused on embedded systems, optimization, and mission-critical software.

I'm a Computer Science senior at Queens College (CUNY) with an associate's degree in Engineering Science. My interests lie in embedded systems, machine learning, optimization, and performance-focused software engineering.

My academic work centers on applying Bayesian Optimization to real-world coverage problems involving mobile observers, time constraints, and spatial-temporal decision making under the supervision of Professor Bon Sy.

My foundation in C++ and Java has shaped a deep interest in low-level, systems-oriented engineering the kind of software that powers aircraft, defense systems, satellites, and other mission-critical technologies.

I approach problems with a systems thinking mindset: understanding constraints, optimizing within them, and building solutions that are reliable by design.

Outside of coursework, I've worked on projects in machine learning, embedded programming, and web development including a React based platform for structured multi-AI collaboration.

Career trajectory: After graduation, I plan to join the Air National Guard and pursue an online MS in Electrical Engineering at the University of Colorado with a long term target of an embedded systems engineering role at Lockheed Martin.
